## `EE301_Transmission_Lines_Full_Explainer.html`

Built bottom-up: what "the wave" actually is, then the toolkit the rest of the course
assumes, then every major result derived from it.

- **Ground floor** — why any of this exists, what a traveling wave actually is
- **The toolkit** — the five things the rest of the course assumes you already have
- **Sections 1–3** — the distributed model, telegrapher's equations, the wave equation and its solution, characteristic impedance
- **Sections 4–6** — launching a pulse, reflection at the load, multiple reflections and the bounce diagram
- **Sections 7–10** — sinusoidal steady state and phasors, standing waves, VSWR, the rotating phasor picture
- **Sections 11–13** — impedance transformation along the line, open/short/stub circuits, power flow
- **Sections 14–16** — lossy lines, lossy resonators and Q factor, quarter-wave impedance matching
- **Sections 17–19** — formula sheet, what they'll actually ask, sign-convention traps and sanity checks

Dark theme, mobile-friendly, works offline.
