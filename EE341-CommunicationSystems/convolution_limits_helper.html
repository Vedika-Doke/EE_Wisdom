Convolution limits helper

Breaking the integral into cases, mechanically — EE341 §3.2

Never guess the cases. Find where a max or a min changes its mind.

Both signals are taken as height 1 here, so the integrand is 1 and the integral is just the length of the overlap. (For heights A and B, multiply everything by A·B at the end.)

The four-step procedure

  1. Write both intervals. x(τ) lives on [a₁, b₁]. And h(t−τ) is nonzero when (t−τ) ∈ [a₂, b₂], which rearranges to τ ∈ [t−b₂, t−a₂]. Note the swap — that is the flip showing up in the limits.
  2. Intersect. lower = max(a₁, t−b₂) and upper = min(b₁, t−a₂). Rightmost left edge, leftmost right edge.
  3. Find the case boundaries. Solve for the t where each max/min switches winner: t = a₁+b₂ and t = b₁+a₂. Add the endpoints where overlap begins and ends: t = a₁+a₂ and t = b₁+b₂. Sort them.
  4. In each region, both winners are fixed — so just subtract: y = upper − lower. Linear in t every time.

Run it

x(τ) nonzero on a₁ b₁
h(τ) nonzero on a₂ b₂
t = 0

Why the number of cases changes

Equal widths → 2 cases. The two switch points a₁+b₂ and b₁+a₂ land on the same t, so there is one boundary and two sloping pieces. That is your rect ∗ rect: 1+t then 1−t.

Unequal widths → 3 cases. The switch points separate, and between them sits a plateau: the narrow block is entirely inside the wide one, so sliding a bit further changes nothing. The overlap is stuck at the narrow width. Try the rect ∗ wide rect preset.

The support is always [a₁+a₂, b₁+b₂] — left ends add, right ends add. That is "widths add", and it is a free check on every answer.

Reading the flip out of the limits

Step 1 above is where the flip hides. For a symmetric h, [a₂,b₂] = [−w/2, w/2] and the interval [t−b₂, t−a₂] comes out symmetric about t — you never notice. For a causal h on [0, w], it becomes [t−w, t]: the block sits entirely to the left of t.

That is causality in picture form — the output at time t only ever looks at inputs from before t. Select the causal preset and watch which side of the marker the orange block stays on.
